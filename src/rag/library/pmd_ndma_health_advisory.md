# National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) & PMD Smog Advisories

## docid#ndma_emergency_advisory
NDMA advises citizens in the Punjab plains to observe precautionary protocols during October-January inversion conditions. Vulnerable individuals, students, and seniors should wear N95 or KN95 particulate respirators outdoors, utilize air purifiers where feasible, and limit commutes during peak morning smog hours.

## docid#ndma_emergency_contacts
For acute respiratory distress or medical emergencies during smog episodes, immediately contact Punjab Emergency Rescue Service (Rescue 1122) or consult the Sehat Sahulat National Health Helpline at 1033.
