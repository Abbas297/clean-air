# WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines (2021)

## docid#who_pm25_annual
For fine particulate matter (PM2.5), the WHO 2021 guideline recommends an annual average concentration not exceeding 5 ug/m3, reflecting updated epidemiological evidence on long-term mortality from cardiopulmonary causes.

## docid#who_pm25_24h
The 24-hour average guideline level for PM2.5 is 15 ug/m3, not to be exceeded more than 3-4 days per year. Short-term exposure to elevated PM2.5 triggers respiratory inflammation, asthma exacerbations, and cardiovascular acute episodes.

## docid#who_interim_targets
Interim target 1 for PM2.5 is 35 ug/m3 (annual) and 75 ug/m3 (24-hour), designated for high-baseline regions transitioning toward cleaner ambient air.

## docid#who_vulnerable_groups
Children, pregnant women, older adults, and individuals with pre-existing chronic cardiopulmonary disease face heightened vulnerability to air pollution. Immediate risk reduction measures include reducing outdoor exertion during peak pollution hours.
