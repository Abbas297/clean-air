# US EPA Air Quality Index (AQI) Technical Guidance

## docid#epa_pm25_breakpoints
The US EPA AQI category breakpoints for 24-hour PM2.5 are defined as follows:
- Good (0 to 50 AQI): 0.0 to 9.0 ug/m3
- Moderate (51 to 100 AQI): 9.1 to 35.4 ug/m3
- Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (101 to 150 AQI): 35.5 to 55.4 ug/m3
- Unhealthy (151 to 200 AQI): 55.5 to 125.4 ug/m3
- Very Unhealthy (201 to 300 AQI): 125.5 to 225.4 ug/m3
- Hazardous (301 to 500 AQI): 225.5 ug/m3 and above.

## docid#epa_action_threshold_55
At 55.5 ug/m3 (AQI 151, Unhealthy threshold), everyone may begin to experience health effects; members of sensitive groups may experience more serious health effects. The EPA recommends that schools reschedule strenuous outdoor activities indoors.
