# Punjab Environmental Protection Department (EPD) Ambient Standards

## docid#punjab_epd_pm25_standard
Under the Punjab Environmental Quality Standards (PEQS), the ambient air quality standard for PM2.5 is established at a 24-hour arithmetic mean of 35 ug/m3 and an annual mean of 15 ug/m3.

## docid#punjab_epd_smog_emergency
When PM2.5 concentrations remain persistently above 150 ug/m3 over multiple consecutive monitoring stations, the Punjab Disaster Management Authority (PDMA) in consultation with EPD issues a Smog Health Emergency notification under Section 4 of the Punjab Environmental Protection Act 1997.

## docid#punjab_epd_school_closures
District Education Authorities (DEAs) across Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, and Multan are mandated to suspend outdoor morning assemblies and physical education classes when forecasted air quality reaches the Unhealthy tier, and transition primary schools to remote learning upon reaching Very Unhealthy tiers (>200 ug/m3).
